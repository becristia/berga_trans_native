BergaTransDash native third-party notices
=========================================

The translation-only native runtime contains Mozilla Translations/Bergamot
inference, Marian NMT, and their pinned transitive dependencies. Exact source
revisions, patches, hashes, license texts, SBOMs, and corresponding-source
archives are published with the Native Release and pinned by
native_artifacts.json.

Marian NMT and the remaining pinned native dependencies retain the individual
license and attribution texts bundled under THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/ in the public
Native package. The consolidated private release-input notice is not copied
into applications by the Build Hook.
